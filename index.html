About

The Face
Behind the Lens

Sidratul Muntaha Ohee is a Dhaka-based fashion model and cosplayer with over two years of modeling experience and seven years of cosplay performance. She has collaborated with emerging fashion brands and photographers, bringing creativity, character expression, and confidence to every project. Her work combines fashion editorial aesthetics with detailed character transformations through cosplay.
